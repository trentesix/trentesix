Devis and Relais have type scales of their own, from the sizes the apps use and the weights their
faces load. Devis: page titles 44px/700, titles 32px/700, body 15px, no heading above 700 (Barlow
Condensed has no 800 or 900, which the browser was faking). Relais: compact headings at 600
(page title 25px, title 28px, section 18px, item 21px), body 15px, small 13px, kbd 500 (the IBM
Plex Mono weight it loads).
